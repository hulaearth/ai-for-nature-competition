## Scope and timing

This package is an organization-card proposal, not a change to the production competition Space. It does not alter the challenge site, GitHub Actions, training data, visibility schedules, scoring, or the 10:00/13:30 release controls. The page may be public ahead of the event; do not paste private guide links or hidden credentials into it.

The `README.md` is the **pre-event** version. Replace it with `README-after-event.md` **after the 24 October 2026 event** when you are ready to present the challenge as an archive, and only after checking the challenge Space remains a usable public archive.

## Publish the card (requires appropriate HulaEarth role)

1. Sign into Hugging Face and open **https://huggingface.co/HulaEarth**.
2. Click **Create organization card** or **Edit organization card**. If a card already exists, first save a copy of its current `README.md`; never overwrite it without comparing content.
3. The card is the `README.md` in the Space **`HulaEarth/README`**. Create it with the built-in organization-card UI if it does not exist.
4. Open the `README` Space **Files** → `README.md` → **Edit**, and paste the complete contents of this package's `README.md` (including its YAML header). If the interface already generated metadata, merge rather than duplicate the YAML header.
5. Make **only** the `HulaEarth/README` Space **Public**. The separate `ai-for-nature-hula-earth` competition Space must retain its intended embargo/visibility policy until the scheduled launch.
6. Open `https://huggingface.co/HulaEarth` in an incognito / signed-out browser. Verify the card displays, headings are readable on mobile, website and GitHub links work, and the challenge link points to the **new owner** `HulaEarth`.
7. Before **10:00 CEST, 24 Oct 2026**, it is expected that an unauthenticated user may be unable to open the competition Space. Do not try to fix this by making the competition Space public early.
8. After 10:00 CEST, check from a signed-out browser that the competition Space opens and its public page is updated. Independently verify the existing 13:30 test-key release, following the production runbook.

## After the event
