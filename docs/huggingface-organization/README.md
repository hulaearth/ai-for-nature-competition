## What we work on

**🔊 Wildlife & bioacoustics**  
AI and autonomous, on-site monitoring to help detect wildlife activity and understand changes in biodiversity.

**🛰️ Earth observation**  
Satellite-derived environmental information combined with field measurements to analyze landscapes and habitats at scale.

**🌱 Biodiversity intelligence**  
Monitoring, reporting, and verification tools that help organizations understand ecological change and make informed decisions.
