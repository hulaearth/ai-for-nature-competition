THE MAIN CHALLENGE / 01

Where could birds thrive?

Birds offer clues about the health of our ecosystems. We chose this challenge to explore how AI can help us understand the habitats they depend on.

Why birds and why habitat?

Every species needs food, water, shelter and space to survive and reproduce. Some birds depend on forests, others on wetlands or open grasslands. Their different needs connect bird observations to the conditions of the surrounding landscape.

At Hula Earth, we want to turn that connection into useful insights for understanding and protecting biodiversity: where could suitable habitat exist, and what can environmental data tell us about it?

Your task: predict habitat suitability

Train a model on German bird observations and the supplied environmental features. Use what it learns to rank anonymous locations in neighboring Central Europe for ten bird species. Run inference on your own compute and submit your predictions; we evaluate them against hidden observation labels.

Observations also reflect where people look and how easily birds are detected. The benchmark ranks reported seasonal presence against matched recording opportunities. Backgrounds are not confirmed absences, and a high score does not establish occupancy or breeding.

Your main modeling work can provide the insights and visualizations for judging: explain your experiments, model behavior and limitations. Tasks 2 and 3 are optional extensions, with no separate requirement or bonus. Any extension work is assessed within the same categories. The provisional public leaderboard is feedback; final performance uses your captain’s chosen submission on the private split. The overall award is out of 100 points. Matching the fixed starter baseline on the private test earns 15 of the 50 model points; beating it by 0.15 AP earns all 50.
