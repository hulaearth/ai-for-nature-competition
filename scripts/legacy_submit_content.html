What happens next

A private GitHub Actions worker validates the uploader, receipt time, encryption, complete keys and quota. It stores a durable private receipt before publishing a sanitized status and aggregate public score. Valid final predictions consume one of five event-wide attempts; practice checks and malformed files do not.

Before opening, final inputs remain encrypted and final submissions are disabled. Uploaders should rehearse practice delivery early. During the event, allow several minutes for scoring; original GitHub timestamps determine eligibility even when the queue is delayed.

FINAL PRESENTATION

Upload your presentation

Use this separate Google Form after preparing your final deck. Sign in with your Google account; the designated team captain submits using the approved team slug and captain GitHub username.

  • Required: one presentation PDF, maximum 10 MB.
  • Optional: one PowerPoint source file, maximum 100 MB.
  • Deadline: Saturday 24 October, 17:30 German time (CEST / MESZ).
  • Presenting: five minutes plus two minutes of questions; slots run 17:45–19:45.

Your files go to organizer-private storage. You do not need permission to edit Challenge data, and other teams cannot browse your slides through the upload form. Complete the upload and press Submit before 17:30; Google Forms is configured to close automatically at that deadline.
