What an environmental location contains

Compact table

A native 10 m embedding vector, circular summaries over 100 m and 1,000 m radii, and three coverage fractions. AlphaEarth contributes 195 predictors; WorldCover adds 39, terrain 19 and historical OSM 36, for 289 in total. The starter joins the companion tables automatically. Context summaries are not single coarse pixels. WorldCover is from 2021 and OSM from January 2024; retain their separate notices.

Local habitat

64 channels × 128 × 128 pixels at 10 m. A 1.28 km footprint, with north-up axes, validity masks and focal-cell offsets.

Wider context

64 channels × 64 × 64 pixels at 40 m. A 2.56 km footprint using Google’s normalized overview. Never average encoded int8 values directly.

Decode valid int8 values using sign(q) × (q / 127.5)²; −128 means NoData. Follow the packaged spatial schema for normalization, masks, axes, focal cells and offsets. National GeoTIFFs retain source CRS and south-up transforms; the released patches are north-up.

Plan final inference around the supplied windows. Both patch scales are available for every final location as compressed NPZ arrays, with the same channels, masks and focal offsets as the German patches. Final inputs have anonymous IDs and relative offsets; continuous georeferenced test-region GeoTIFFs are not supplied. A model trained from Germany’s whole rasters must therefore compute its final features from these windows. Check this before choosing features that require a larger area or absolute coordinates.

Optional climate, water and landscape context

The climate companion supplies 12 CHELSA 1981–2010 normals. The water and landscape companion supplies 77 fine-water, broad land-cover/water and coast predictors. Matching values cover German training, development and helpers, practice, every anonymous final location, and the prepared 1 km / 5 km mapping grids. With both optional companions, there are 378 numeric predictors. The fixed reference remains the 289-feature model.

German companions are unencrypted. Test companions use the same 13:30 event key; use a separate extraction directory for each. Water/context is 6.0 MB for Germany and 1.1 MB for the encrypted test. These are keyed summaries, not extra image channels. Retain JRC credit “Source: EC JRC/Google”, WorldCover CC BY 4.0 attribution, and the climate and coastline notices. See the package dictionaries and the starter’s environmental-input instructions.

Development and optional CV

Report the required German development AP using the supplied labels and exclusions. Fit scaling, imputation, dimensionality reduction, feature selection and supervised auxiliary stages inside each training partition. The same spatial and source exclusions apply across species.

Optional five-fold spatial CV is useful for comparing one or two promising models; it is not required and earns no points merely for being run. For cross-species claims, present paired results with and without other species on identical focal labels, backgrounds, splits and feature supports.

A final refit on German training plus development is allowed after model selection. Declare it and retain the earlier held-out development score. Additional released German labels must follow the documented season, quality, site and buffer rules.

Final predictions and metric

Submit exactly 63,590 rows using row_id,score. Predict all ten species at all 6,359 requested locations. Keys must exactly match the decrypted sample submission; order does not matter. Scores must be finite numbers in [0,1]. A score is a ranking output, not a calibrated occupancy probability.

For each concealed partition, pool all scored countries within each species, calculate average precision, then average all ten species equally. Higher is better. Background ratio and eligibility are frozen. Unknown labels are excluded; they are not converted into negatives.

The provisional board displays only aggregate public macro AP. AP comparisons use full precision; scores are displayed to six decimals. An exact AP tie is broken by the earlier relevant accepted prediction timestamp, then by stable entry reference if receipt times are identical. On the final board this is the nominated prediction’s original receipt time. The final board uses the geographically separate private split. Neither private scores nor group details are released during model selection. Geographic identifiers and public/private membership are absent from test inputs.

Model points: improve on a fixed reference

Matching the shipped reference earns 15/50 model points. An improvement of +0.15 AP earns 50/50. Points are capped at 0 and 50.

Let s be your chosen prediction’s final private AP, a = 1/6 the constant-score AP and b the frozen reference model’s AP on that same private split. For s ≤ b, points are max(0, 15 × (s − a) / (b − a)). For s > b, points are min(50, 15 + 35 × (s − b) / 0.15). At or below constant-score AP you earn zero model points.

Questions teams often ask

Why train on Germany instead of all Europe?

The learning and exploration release has a clear German boundary. Final evaluation tests geographic transfer to nearby Central Europe while foreign bird observations and exact evaluation locations stay with the organizers. Every team receives the same permitted data.

Will a city park disappear in a coarse average?

It can. The native vector and 10 m local patch retain finer environmental detail, while context summaries and wider patches describe the landscape. Compare supports rather than assuming one resolution is best. Fine pixels do not remove uncertainty in the reported bird position.

Are 100 m and 1 km features single coarse pixels?

No. They summarize circular neighborhoods of those radii. Native vectors, context summaries, 10 m patches and 40 m patches are different environmental supports; the data schemas explain each representation.

Can I use an ordinary image model?

You can develop spatial architectures, but inputs have 64 environmental channels and validity masks, not RGB colors. Apply documented decoding and normalization. Generic brightness augmentation or ImageNet color preprocessing has no automatic meaning here.

Must I train five models for CV?

No. Everyone reports the common German development result; five supplied spatial folds are optional. Learned preprocessing and helper stages must still obey the partition and exclusion rules.

Can I refit on training plus development?

Yes, after model selection and with a declaration. Retain the earlier honest held-out development score and use the same final inference interface.

Can I download additional bird observations?

No. Additional bird labels, range maps and pretrained bird-distribution predictions are prohibited. Teams may obtain additional environmental data for analysis, visualization and training. Final predictions must be computable from the supplied anonymous test inputs; organizers will not prepare additional test features on request. Use supplied bird observations only. Retrieving external bird observations, species range maps or pretrained bird-distribution predictions, identifying test locations, or retrieving evaluation labels is prohibited.

Does background zero mean the bird was absent?

No. It is a matched recording opportunity sampled for this benchmark. Unreported species remain unknown. Scores rank reported seasonal presence against that fixed background rather than measuring calibrated occupancy.

How can other species help at final locations?

Learn transferable helper signals inside the permitted training partitions and predict them from the supplied final inputs. Observed helper labels at final locations are not supplied. Use out-of-fold or another leakage-safe construction and a matched control.

What if my scoring receipt arrives after the deadline?

The original unedited Hugging Face server receipt binds the exact request and ciphertext fingerprint. Its timestamp controls eligibility, even if scoring finishes later. Keep the receipt, avoid edits and allow time to check acceptance.

Is the private score selected from my best attempt?

No. Final performance uses one chosen accepted prediction; the latest accepted captain upload is the fallback; if only teammates uploaded, the captain must choose an entry. Five accepted final predictions apply across the whole event, not per day.

Do slide uploads need dataset editing permission?

No. The presentation Form routes files to private organizer storage. Challenge data remains a download-only folder for participants. Open it below prediction submission in the Submit tab. It requires a Google account; keep its confirmation. No separate GitHub nomination is required.
